<Myth busters>

What FDE is not

Myth 01
"It's just pre-sales or solutions consulting with a new name."
Reality
An FDE ships production code inside the client's stack, measured on deployment outcomes - not staffed against a billable-utilisation target.
Myth 02
"Embedded means relocating to the customer's office."
Reality
Embedded is hybrid, not relocation.
Myth 03
"It's entry-level delivery work."
Reality
73% of disclosed job descriptions are mid or senior. Structurally, this is a mid-senior engineering role.
Myth 04
"You need to be an ML researcher to do this."
Reality
~70-80% skill overlap with AI engineering. Models aren't the bottleneck - deployment is. You don't need to train the model; you need to land it.
Myth 05
"It's a US-only role that hasn't reached India."
Reality
TCS is building a forward-deployed practice of 5,900-8,900 engineers - competing directly with OpenAI, Anthropic and Microsoft for the same talent.
Every myth comes from the same root - people assume the title is new marketing. The shape of the work is ten years old. What changed is the demand.

<CHAPTER 02>

Who is an FDE?

Role. Engagement.

<The simplest definition>

An FDE sits where three things overlap

AI Engineer

Agents, RAG, evals, guardrails - you know how AI systems behave, and how they fail in production.

Customer Facing

You can sit with a client, find the real problem behind the stated one, and be trusted inside their environment.

Solutions Architect

You can design a system that fits their constraints, integrations and compliance - not a reference diagram.

Most engineers have one or two. The FDE has all three - that is the whole scarcity.
