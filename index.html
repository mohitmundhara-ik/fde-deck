<CHAPTER 02>

Who is an FDE?

Role. Engagement.

<The simplest definition>

An FDE sits where three things overlap

AI Engineer

Agents, RAG, evals, guardrails - you know how AI systems behave, and how they fail in production.

Customer Facing

You can sit with a client, find the real problem behind the stated one, and be trusted inside their environment.

Solutions Architect

You can design a system that fits their constraints, integrations and compliance - not a reference diagram.

Most engineers have one or two. The FDE has all three - that is the whole scarcity.

An FDE hire is a risk decision, not a skills checklist.

They are putting you inside a paying customer's environment, largely alone. So they screen for three fears - in this order.

FEAR 01 "Can they build it?"
82%
of FDE job descriptions require AI/ML

Table stakes - and the easiest of the three to prove.

FEAR 02 "Will they survive the customer?"
99%
customer-facing · 92% embedded

The one most engineers have zero evidence for.

FEAR 03 "Will it still run after they leave?"
~95%
of pilots died before production

They have been burned before - that is why the role exists.

The bar is not more years - it is evidence across all three. That is why a 4-year engineer with one deployed system beats a 12-year engineer with none.
